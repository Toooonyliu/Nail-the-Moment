# Hand model

`generic-hand-left.glb` is the `generic-hand` left-hand asset from the Immersive Web Community Group's WebXR Input Profiles repository.

- Source: https://github.com/immersive-web/webxr-input-profiles/blob/main/packages/assets/profiles/generic-hand/left.glb
- Downloaded: 2026-10-19
- License: MIT; complete original notice is in `LICENSE-webxr.md`.
- Asset overview: https://github.com/immersive-web/webxr-input-profiles/blob/main/packages/assets/README.md

The binary asset is unchanged. The prototype applies a skin material and orientation at runtime, with separately generated editable curved nail shells and 3D nail-art pieces. It represents a generic demo hand, not a reconstruction of an uploaded photo.
